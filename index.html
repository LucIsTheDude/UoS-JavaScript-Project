My Robot

How to operate the robot:

To start, press the "Power On" button. This will turn the robot on and will allow you to click on the canvas to perform actions. You can move the robot by clicking on the canvas anywhere at leg level or below. You can also make the robot spin either of it's arms for a few seconds by clicking on them. Finally, if you click anywhere else on the canvas, the robot will fire lasers at that location. If you want to charge the robot, press the "Charge Robot" button, this will charge the robot for a few seconds back to full power. You can also power the robot off whenever you want by pressing the "Power Off" button.

My robot software works on all plaforms and screen sizes as the canvas and elemets scale appropriately. This includes mobile devices, tablets, and desktops.
